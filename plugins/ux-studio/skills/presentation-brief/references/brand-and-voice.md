# Brand and voice

## Client brand vs neutral brand

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Client supplied logo, colors, fonts AND approved their use | Use them, cite the approval in BRIEF.md section 4. |
| Client assets missing or approval not explicit | Neutral consultancy brand. Say why in the brief. |
| Client look only inferred from a public site | Treat as a reference for the KEEP-CHANGE-ADD-DROP audit, not as permission to reproduce marks. |
| Third-party proprietary marks (logos, trademarked fonts or icons) | Never reproduce. Use generic equivalents. |

Always state in the showcase and videos that the prototype's look is not the final design. Reuse an existing design system before creating one.

## Mapping to design-system-builder

| Brief brand path | design-system-builder path |
|---|---|
| Reuse existing | reuse the existing claude.ai Design System artifact |
| From code | extract tokens and components from the codebase |
| From existing website | screenshots or URL, with KEEP-CHANGE-ADD-DROP audit |
| From scratch | neutral consultancy brand, minimal token set |

Pass to the builder: path, brand (client or neutral), tokens known, logo permission, voice, motion and sonic stance.

## Voice and tone

- Write in the audience's real vocabulary (operators: their daily terms; executives: outcomes).
- Choose address form once (tuteo / voseo / usted) per locale and keep it across tour, captions and slides.
- Tone words: pick 3 (e.g. clear, calm, direct). Record them in the brief.

## Motion as part of the brand

- Motion belongs to the brand system, not decoration. Reference: https://brand.oscars.org/motion
- Method: choose motion brand words first, then settle easing and duration by experiment. Reference: https://bootcamp.uxdesign.cc/building-a-motion-design-system-for-a-product-company-307b34d051e4
- Extend tokens with motion: duration scale, easing curves, entrance/exit patterns, transition rules between scenes.
- Honour reduced motion in the interactive pieces; videos need a calm variant when requested.

## Sonic identity

Decide and record:
- Logo sting: yes / no.
- Voice: synthetic or human, gender-neutral option, pace, language variant.
- Music mood: or none. Never music over the voice in trainer clips.
- Loudness target: state one (e.g. consistent integrated level across all videos).

Captions are mandatory regardless of sound choices.
